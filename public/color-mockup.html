❌ Strict "Do Not" Rules for Developers
  • × No Bright Yellow/Gold: Do NOT use bright yellow gold (#D4AF37, #FFD700). Use only Muted Champagne (#C5A880) or Warm Bronze (#A68A64).
  • × No Colored Table Hovers: Table row hover must strictly remain Whisper Gray (#F9FAFB). No golden, blue or yellow rows on hover.
  • × No Colored Card Backgrounds: All KPI cards, content areas and modals must stay Pure White (#FFFFFF).
  • × No Heavy Logos: Keep the PAX TV logo transparent PNG without enclosed heavy contrast backgrounds.
✔ Approved Best Practices
  • ✓ Active Menu Tab: Always apply a 3px solid #C5A880 indicator line at the left edge with a subtle dark background contrast.
  • ✓ KPI Icon Enclosures: Keep icons inside an 8px rounded container filled with Warm Sand (#F5F2EB) and Deep Bronze (#8C7250) icon fill.
  • ✓ Hairline Borders: Use 1px solid #E5E7EB for cards, tables, and top headers to establish clean luxury spacing.
  • ✓ Secondary Buttons: Keep them clean with Pure White background, Hairline Gray border, and Dark Slate text.
